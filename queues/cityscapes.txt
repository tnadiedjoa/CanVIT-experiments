# Cityscapes at its native resolution (scripts/cityscapes.py): linear probes trained on the training split, then
# mIoU on the 1000 validation half-images at 1024 px.
#
# Canvas probe with the paper's protocol (512 px scenes, 64×64 canvas, 10 R-IID glimpses), then C2F down to level 3
# (85 glimpses) with glimpses from the 512 px scene, from the 1024 px scene for level 3 only, and from the 1024 px
# scene throughout. The first two share their first 21 glimpses exactly (same seed, same tile order).
probes/cityscapes_canvas_s512_c64.pt scripts/cityscapes.py train-probe --features canvas --scene-size-px 512 --canvas-grid-size 64
results/cityscapes/c2f_c64_t85_s512_s0.json scripts/cityscapes.py rollouts --probe probes/cityscapes_canvas_s512_c64.pt --detail s512
results/cityscapes/c2f_c64_t85_s512_then_native_s0.json scripts/cityscapes.py rollouts --probe probes/cityscapes_canvas_s512_c64.pt --detail s512_then_native
results/cityscapes/c2f_c64_t85_native_s0.json scripts/cityscapes.py rollouts --probe probes/cityscapes_canvas_s512_c64.pt --detail native
# EG-C2F: within each level, the tile of highest probe entropy first, so the curve over level 3 shows how many of
# its 64 glimpses capture the gain of real detail.
results/cityscapes/egc2f_c64_t85_s512_then_native.json scripts/cityscapes.py rollouts --probe probes/cityscapes_canvas_s512_c64.pt --detail s512_then_native --policy entropy_coarse_to_fine
results/cityscapes/egc2f_c64_t85_s512.json scripts/cityscapes.py rollouts --probe probes/cityscapes_canvas_s512_c64.pt --detail s512 --policy entropy_coarse_to_fine
