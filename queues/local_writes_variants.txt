# Writes also kept in the cells that no glimpse has covered yet. With plain local Writes, the policies that start
# with small glimpses (R-IID, F2C) lose early, because such a cell then receives nothing.
riid_c64_t85_s0_unseen1 --policy random --num-glimpses 85 --canvas-grid-size 64 --local-writes-from 1 --unseen-writes
f2c_c64_t21_s0_unseen1 --policy fine_to_coarse --num-glimpses 21 --canvas-grid-size 64 --local-writes-from 1 --unseen-writes
# Writes also kept within half a crop width of the crop, where the leak is strongest. From level 3: is the harmful part
# of the leak the far one? From glimpse 1: does the near leak carry what level 1 needs (plain local Writes from
# glimpse 1 cost 0.4 points at t = 20)?
c2f_c64_t85_s0_local21_margin0.5 --policy coarse_to_fine --num-glimpses 85 --canvas-grid-size 64 --local-writes-from 21 --local-writes-margin 0.5
c2f_c64_t85_s0_local1_margin0.5 --policy coarse_to_fine --num-glimpses 85 --canvas-grid-size 64 --local-writes-from 1 --local-writes-margin 0.5
